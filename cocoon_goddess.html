Hall of the Weeping Violinist

Statue: a statue of a weeping woman with widow's bellflowers woven into her hair stands atop a 10ft wide 1ft high dais to the south. This is a teleportation statue [DETAILS GO HERE].

Portcullis: a portcullis stands at the east entrance. It is operated by a lever on the northeast wall. Normally the portcullis is left down to prevent wandering creatures entering the Second Society.

Exits: the southeast door has an inverted U drawn on it in chalk. The northwest corridor has an inverted U on the ground at the entrance, and smells strongly of smoke.

Chamber of Smokey Apparitions

Chimney: smoke pours from a 3ft square hole in the centre of the chamber floor, leading directly to the [CREMATORIUM] below.

Smoke: the entire room is filled with thick choking smoke, causing 1hp damage per round to those inhaling it. Covering one's mouth and nose reduces this to 1hp every other round, while those with lung damage take double damage. Wailing faces can occasionally be glimpsed in the shifting whorls of smoke.

Summoning apparitions: burning a corpse in the [CREMATORIUM] summons an apparition of the creature in the smoke here lasting for one turn. The apparition cannot move or attack, but can be communicated with as though it were alive.

Crypts of Clemency

Coffins: simple stone coffins line both the northwest and southeast walls. All of them lie open and already looted, containing nothing but shriveled corpses wrapped in funerary shrouds. The lids bear names, dates (all more than 800 years ago), and causes of death, usually disease or injury; these were patients of the Well of Clemency who died in the course of treatment.

Ventilation shaft: to the west, at floor height, a horizontal stone shaft 1.5ft tall, 2ft wide shaft extends into the rock. It lies covered by one of the coffins and not immediately obvious from the entrance.

East Atrium

Balconies: 20ft up, there are balconies with 2ft balustrades overlooking the lower floor on the north, east, and west sides.

Triptych mural: three old faded murals cover the southwest, west, and northwest walls at the ground floor.

Three Crypts

Northeast door: marked with a red hand. No doorhandle or keyhole. A plaque above the door reads "Until his return" in Ecclesiastic, with a translation in Common Imperial written in chalk underneath.

East door: locked. A stylised swan with its neck tied in a knot is emblazoned on the door. A symbol of a key is drawn beside it in chalk.

Southeast door: slightly ajar, has clearly been forced open. An inverted U is drawn on it in chalk.

Vernon's Repose

Urn: a 5ft tall ornate terracotta urn decorated with geometric patterns in lapis lazuli, worth around 2500gp, stands on a pedestal to the northeast. It contains the ashes of Patriarch Vernon, former high priest of the Hand Incarnadine. Shortly before death his consciousness was transferred into the urn by means of a Magic Jar spell, in case he is needed again. He remains there in a state of dormancy until the urn is touched, at which point he will attempt to possess whoever is nearest.

Patriarch Vernon: he is a 9th level cleric with the following spells prepared: Cure Light Wounds × 2, Remove Fear; Steel Suture × 2, Hold Person; Cure Disease × 2, Umbilical Cord; Cure Serious Wounds, Neutralise Poison; Raise Dead × 2. He is disgusted by possessing a disabled person and demands an abled body to possess instead. He is also horrified to learn The Hand Incarnadine is dead—note this means he is unable to prepare more spells once his current ones are cast. Nonetheless he will abide by his vows and heal the sick to the best of his ability.

Door: opens normally from this side (with doorhandle).

X of Swords

Coffin: an open coffin in the centre of the room contains a contorted corpse impaled by ten swords. Nine are ordinary nonmagical silver swords (100gp each) while the tenth is the solid silver blade of the [SWORD].

Corpse: the corpse is actually a Greater Husk, its regeneration impeded by the swords impaling it. If any of the swords are removed it reanimates after a number of rounds equal to the remaining swords. [BACKSTORY GOES HERE]

Looted Tomb

Sarcophagus: a stone sarcophagus lies in the centre of the room. Slumped over one side is the remains of a mummy, hacked to pieces and with its face badly burned, having evidently animated and subsequently been slain. Nothing of value remains.

Hall of Signs

Northwest door: spiked shut from this side and marked with a large X in chalk.

Northeast door: a stylised hand is drawn on the wall nearby with an arrow pointing to the door.

South entrance: a pile of blocks suggesting a stylised cityscape is drawn on the wall nearby with an arrow pointing up the stairs.

Hungry Graveyard

Graveyard: the ground here is soft clotting grave dirt, dotted with crooked tombstones with faded inscriptions.

Sinking: the grave dirt sucks anything heavy down like quicksand, where it sinks through the ground into the Council of Vermin. Victims grasping at the tombstones will only pull the tombstone through with them. This unenviable fate can be avoided by use of levitation, sprinting through the room, jumping from tombstone to tombstone, wearing snowshoes, etc.

An Inauspicious Sight

Bloodstains: splatters of dried blood coat the centre of the room, with a large smear suggesting a body dragged to the northwest.

Ghouls: three ghouls arrive from the Demesne of the Ghoul Princess in 1d3 rounds to greet guests. They will attempt to circle around to prevent retreat and corral guests towards the princess.

Burial niches line all the walls from floor to ceiling. Searching them thoroughly takes six turns and yields 12gp in coins. One niche contains a partially animated skeletal hand that grasps at anything that moves (startling, but harmless).

Demesne of the Ghoul Princess

Ghoul princess: a Ghoul wearing a rose gold tiara worth 1200gp, tattered remains of silk robes, and wielding a femur as a sceptre. The only thing she loves more than entertaining guests is feasting upon human flesh. She speaks High Imperial only.

Ghoul courtiers: three Ghouls are here prostrating themselves before the princess, while a fourth is on all fours being used as her throne. They unquestioningly obey anyone wearing the princess's tiara. They understand High Imperial but do not speak.

Gore: shreds of dessicated flesh and dried blood coat the floor where previous guests have been cannibalised. Piles of shattered bones lie swept into the corners.

Treasure: around the princess's feet are piled 780gp in coins, 300gp in gems, a set of studded leather armour +2, a glaive, two throwing axes +1, a Crest of the Falcon, and a scroll case containing scrolls of Dimension Door, Growth of Plants, and Glyph (Petrification).

Burial niches line all the walls from floor to ceiling. Searching them thoroughly takes six turns and yields 6gp in coins and two swords rusted beyond use. One niche contains a crude shrine to the Yearning Maw set up by the princess.

Banquet of Doom

Banquet table: a simple wooden table holds a sumptuous banquet, including bowls of fruit, stuffed quail, a leg of lamb, a whole roasted suckling pig, etc. In reality this is simply an illusion cast over a pile of rotting maggot-ridden meat. Food taken from the table retains its illusion for six turns. Missing food is magically replaced with more on the table when unobserved.

Eating from the banquet: it is delicious and causes no immediate harm. After six turns the eater must save vs spells. On a success they get away with just being incapacitated by gastrointestinal illness for 2d12 days. On a failure, their skin turns pallid, their eyes go dim, and their phalanges burst through their fingertips as raking claws, as they are transformed into a Ghoul!

Ghouls: the ghouls from Demesne of the Ghoul Princess are aware of the secret door and will come in 1d3 rounds if they hear significant noise. They do not eat from the banquet; due to its enchanted nature it provides no sustenance to them.

Burial niches line all the walls from floor to ceiling. Searching them thoroughly takes six turns and yields 10gp in coins and a silver ring worth 25gp.

Five Boxed Wizards

Coffins: five simple stone coffins line the southwest wall.

Alter Memory (Illusionist 1, Magic-User 2)

Duration: Permanent

Range: Caster

By means of this spell, the caster can alter their own memory to sincerely convince themselves of anything. If used to remove a phobia or delusion, the caster must save vs spells, with failure indicating that the belief is too deeply ingrained to be affected by this spell.

Blunt (Magic-User 1)

Duration: Permanent

Range: 90ft

A sharp object in range is blunted, causing its damage to be halved. If the object is in the possession of a creature (e.g. a wielded sword or a monster's teeth) the creature may save vs spells to resist. On a success, the duration is reduced to 1d6 rounds.

Cannibalistic Frenzy (Cleric 1)

Duration: 1 round per level

Range: 90ft

A single creature gains +1 to hit, +3 damage, and 5 temporary hit points. They must attempt to bite and eat the nearest creature. Unwilling targets may save vs spells to resist.

Chromatic Beam (Illusionist 1)

Duration: Instant

Range: 60ft

This spell is identical to Chromatic Orb, except that it takes the visual form of a coloured laser beam instead of an orb.

Conjure Persimmons (Druid 1)

Duration: Instant

Range: 30ft

If this spell is cast targetting open ground, a sapling sprouts bearing 1d6 delicious ripe persimmons. If targetting a tree, the tree bears 5d6 delicious ripe persimmons, irrespective of species. Otherwise the spell fails. Four persimmons are equivalent to one ration of food.

Continual Silence (Cleric 3)

Duration: Permanent

Range: 180ft

A 30ft radius area is rendered utterly silent. Conversation and spellcasting are impossible in the area, but noise from outside can be heard. Unlike Silence 15ft Radius it may not be cast on a creature. The reversed version of this spell cancels an area of Continual Silence.

Damnatio Memoriae (Cleric 5, Magic-User 6)

Duration: Instant

Range: Caster

This spell permanently erases one creature, object, or (at referee's discretion) concept from existence. The subject vanishes, text or images of the subject become blank, memories of the subject are erased, etc. The effect is not retroactive, that is, past events involving the subject still happened, but memories or records of them will have conspicuous gaps where the subject is involved. Once cast, the spell itself is similarly erased and can never again be researched or cast by anyone.

Dance, Worms! (Illusionist 3, Magic-User 3)

Duration: 1 round per level

Range: 60ft

Up to 2HD of creatures per level of caster must save vs spells or be compelled to dance for the duration. Dancing creatures suffer a -2 penalty to AC and saves, and have a 2-in-6 chance for any spellcasting or attack to fail.

Detect Good (Magic-User 2)

Duration: 2 turns

Range: 60ft

This spell is identical to Detect Evil but detects good instead.

Dominate Undead (Cleric 4, Magic-User 4)

Duration: 1 turn

Range: 30ft

A number of HD of undead equal to the caster's level come under the caster's complete control.

Dryad Step (Druid 3)

Duration: Instant

Range: Touch

The caster may step into a tree and reappear at any other tree they have touched before. They may bring one additional creature with them for every level above 5th.

Exile (Cleric 2)

Duration: 1 week per level

Range: 10ft per level

This spell affects an area with a radius up to 10ft per level. A single creature in range must save vs spells or be compelled to leave the area and be unable to willingly return for the duration. Mindless or undead creatures are unaffected.

Fallow (Cleric 1, Druid 1)

Duration: Instant

Range: 30ft

If the target creature is bearing a child, the pregnancy is terminated. Unwilling creatures may save vs spells to resist. A caster of fifth level or above may apply an additional effect, permanently sterilising the target creature.

Go Away (Magic-User 5)

Duration: Permanent

Range: 60ft

Casting this spell requires a gemstone or crystal worth at least 10,000gp. A target creature is banished to a featureless demiplane of roiling mist and strange disembodied light. After one hour the target may save vs spells, and on a success they return to the nearest unoccupied space to where they previously were. While in the demiplane creatures' bodily processes are suspended and they can survive indefinitely. If the spell is cast on multiple creatures using the same crystal, they are all sent to the same demiplane. If the crystal is destroyed, all creatures return to this plane of existence.

Glyph (Cleric 4, Magic-User 3)

Duration: Permanent until triggered

Range: Touch

Using a ritual taking one hour and powdered gems worth 500gp, an object or section of wall or floor is marked with an invisible glyph. If the glyph is touched by a creature, they must save vs spells or suffer some effect, according to the specific glyph. Possible glyphs include:

  • Blinding (permanent)
  • Paralysis (permanent)
  • Banishment (extradimensional beings save or be banished to their home plane, no effect on other creatures)
  • Smiting (electrocuted for 8d6 damage, save for half)
  • Death (instant death, no effect on undead)
  • Petrification

Holy Vow (Cleric 2)

Duration: Until vow is fulfilled or failed

Range: Caster

The caster vows to their deity to accomplish a quest in their name, for example to slay an enemy, rescue a captive, or avenge a fallen companion. They gain +1 to all rolls directly furthering the goal. The caster may only commit themself to one holy vow at a time. If the caster gives up or becomes unable to fulfil the vow, they lose all spellcasting powers until this is atoned for.

Human Charm (Cleric 2, Druid 2)

Duration: 1d4+1 rounds or turns

Range: 60ft

One or more humans are rendered non-hostile, standing straight and swaying to and fro but not attacking.

  • HD affected: The spell affects humans whose total Hit Dice do not exceed the caster’s level. For example, a 7th level caster can affect 7 HD of humans, which can equal seven 1 HD humans, or two 3 HD huamns and one 1 HD human, or any other combination.
  • Duration: When cast on humans that are already attacking, the spell lasts for 1d4+1 rounds. Otherwise, it lasts for 1d4+1 turns.

Illusory Wall (Illusionist 2, Magic-User 4)

Duration: Permanent

Range: 30ft

This spell conjures a featureless illusory wall across a doorway or other opening, no more than 10ft in any dimension. The wall superficially matches the surrounding architecture and stonework.

Knotted Fates (Druid 3, Magic-User 4)

Duration: Permanent

Range: 30ft

This spell binds the fates of the caster and subject, such that if one dies then the other dies simultaneously. An unwilling subject may save vs spells to negate the effect. An affected subject intuitively understands the effects of the spell even if they do not share a language with the caster.

Little Quentin (Illusionist 1, Magic-User 1)

Duration: Instant

Range: Caster

This bizarre creation of an eccentric psychomancer is a sentient spell. While prepared but not yet cast, Little Quentin manifests as a voice in the back of the user's head. He is shy but friendly and desperately wishes to be liked. Little Quentin may cast Detect Magic three times a day and can identify the general properties of any magic item held by the user for at least a turn. Unfortunately, he is terrified of danger or death. In the case of combat or other mortal peril, the user must save vs spells or be compelled to flee for one turn.

Little Quentin may only be prepared by one caster at a time. If cast, the spell does nothing but create a dissonant screeching noise that deafens all within 30ft for 1d4 rounds, and may attract wandering monsters.

Mass Water Breathing (Druid 4, Magic-User 4)

Duration: 1 day

Range: 30ft

Up to 25 creatures in range gain the ability to breathe underwater for the duration.

Mazarine's Brain Explosion (Magic-User 2)

Duration: 3 rounds

Range: 30ft

The caster builds pressure inside the subject's skull. For the duration they suffer -1 to all rolls due to pain, and at the end of the third round, their brain explodes killing them instantly. If the caster's concentration is broken or the subject exits the range then the spell ends prematurely. Undead and creatures without brains are unaffected.

Mute (Cleric 1, Magic-User 1)

Duration: 1 turn

Range: 30ft

A single creature is unable to create any sound for the duration. An unwilling subject may save vs spells to resist.

Purifying Flame (Cleric 4, Druid 5)

Duration: Concentration

Range: Touch

A willing creature is engulfed in white spectral flame. Every round, they take 1d8 damage and recover one point each of INT, WIS, and CHA damage. If the damage is in any way blocked or reduced (e.g. by Resist Fire) the spell fails.

Scapegoat (Illusionist 3, Druid 3)

Duration: Instant

Range: Touch

Using rare magical herbs worth at least 500gp, the caster crafts a straw doll in the likeness of the subject. If the subject is under the effects of a curse, the curse is "tricked" into transferring to the doll. Alternatively, the doll can be worn around the neck to "catch" curses cast on the subject. Once cursed, the doll must immediately (within one round) be destroyed using magical fire or else the curse will transfer back to the subject in a more severe form.

Senseless (Illusionist 4, Magic-User 5)

Duration: 1 turn

Range: Caster

All creatures in a 60ft radius of the caster lose use of all their senses for the duration. They are blinded, deafened, and cannot sense touch, position, or pain. For those who pass a save vs spells, the duration is reduced to 1d6 rounds. Affected creatures will usually fall over and writhe in confusion for the duration, but those aware of and expecting the effect may be able to take slow, careful, pre-planned actions.

Steel Suture (Cleric 2)

Duration: Instant

Range: 30ft

This spell has three uses.

  • Closing wounds: The spell restores 1d6+1 HP to a creature, provided they lost that HP within the last turn from a sharp flesh wound.
  • Closing apertures: The spell sutures closed a doorway, window, or other aperture with sharp, taut steel wire. If cut, the severed ends whip out causing 1d8 damage to any creature within 5ft that fails a save vs wands.
  • Closing orifices: The spell sutures closed a target's eyelids (causing blindness), lips (preventing speech), or other orifice. An unwilling subject may save vs spells to resist. Sutures take one hour of careful work to remove without causing serious tissue damage.

Stones to Children (Cleric 4, Druid 4)

Duration: 6 turns

Range: 120ft

2d8 normal stones are miraculously transformed into human children that follow the caster's orders.

  • Reversion: When killed or at the end of the spell's duration, the children revert back into stones.
  • Conjured children: use the statblock of a Normal Human, and can bite for 1d2 damage. They have no names or personalities and do not speak any language.

If the true name of a demon is used in the ritual then that demon is summoned, otherwise it is random. The demon is not beholden to the caster in any way and will probably immediately attack them.

Umbilical Cord (Cleric 3, Druid 3)

Duration: 1 round per level

Range: 10ft

This spell conjures a bloody cord of flesh tethering together the caster and target. Unwilling subjects may save vs spells to resist. Each round, the caster may transfer 1d6 hit points to, or drain 1d6 hit points from, the target. The subject and caster cannot move more than 10ft from one another. The cord may be attacked and has AC 9 [10] and 2 hit points. Using this spell to drain health from an unwilling target is a chaotic act.

Wall of Slime (Magic-User 4)

Duration: Concentration

Range: 60ft

An immobile, opaque curtain of Green Slime springs into existence.

  • Size: The wall can be any size, and may be shaped in any manner and to any dimensions the caster desires, up to 1,200 sq. ft. For example, it may be a straight wall or curved into a protective circle.
  • Location: The wall may not be evoked so that it appears where objects are.
  • Slime: the acidic slime destroys wood, metal, and flesh in 6 rounds.
  • Destroying: a 10ft by 10ft section of wall can be destroyed by 9hp of fire or cold damage.
  • Concentration: The wall persists as long as the caster concentrates on the spell and does not move.

Water of Life (Cleric 5, Druid 4)

Range: Touch

A willing creature is fully immersed in a natural body of water while the caster chants over them. They recover one point of STR, CON, and DEX damage every hour. This usually requires Water Breathing or at least a snorkel, in addition to some way to stave off hypothermia.

Amulets of Reunion

Value: 2000gp together

Charges: 1d6 (collectively, between both)

A pair of identical pewter amulets. The wearers can always telepathically hear the thoughts of one another. Furthermore, the wearer of one may expend a charge to teleport to the location of the other.

Armour of Sir Umbral

Value: 8000gp

A suit of void-black full plate armour +1, granting AC 1 [18]. The wearer gains infravision to 60ft and a 5-in-6 chance to hide in shadows.

Battlestaff of the Cube

Value: 500gp per charge

Charges: 1d8

An unremarkable looking gnarled piece of driftwood. The wielder may expend a charge to summon a Gelatinous Cube somewhere within 90ft for one turn. The cube cannot be commanded and will just mindlessly try to absorb nearby organic matter.

Black Scroll

Value: 200gp per level

A spell scroll completely saturated in ink, unreadable by normal means. Read Magic can identify the spell, but only if the caster is blind or has their eyes covered. The scroll may be cast by any spellcaster, arcane or divine, but cannot be reversed.

Blessed Ring of Protection

Value: 5000gp

A simple ring carved from tortoiseshell. It grants +3 to AC and saves, and if the wearer would die from hit point loss, the ring shatters and they are left alive on 1hp instead.

Blessed Shadow-Candle

Value: 900gp

A grey candle inlaid with gold. If lit, it can be used to cast the 5th level Illusionist spell Shadowcast, including sound. The gold inlay, if extracted from the candle, is worth 200gp.

Blooming Bough

Value: 1800gp

What appears to be a delicate plum blossom branch is actually a club +1. Creatures struck by it must save vs spells or be charmed for 1d4 rounds (as Charm Monster). Flowers in the wielder's possession will never wilt.

Bolt of Explosion

Value: 300gp

An enchanted +1 crossbow bolt. On hit, it creates an explosion dealing 4d6 damage in a 20ft radius, halved on a successful save vs breath weapons.

Bolt of Freezing

Value: 200gp

An enchanted +1 crossbow bolt. On hit, it causes extreme cold in a 20ft radius, inflicting 1d8 damage, freezing any liquid water for one turn, and covering any solid ground in a film of black ice.

Bolt of Healing

Value: 100gp

An enchanted +1 crossbow bolt. On hit, it heals any creature struck for 3d8hp instead of inflicting damage.

Bolt of Revelation

Value: 150gp

An enchanted +1 crossbow bolt. On hit, it creates a 30ft radius bright light that lasts 6 turns. Invisible creatures and illusions in the radius are revealed.

Bolt of Teleportation

Value: 100gp

An enchanted +1 crossbow bolt. On hit, it teleports the creature that fired it to the location struck.

Brainflayer

Value: 14,000gp

A horrifying three-headed demonic whip that shreds the mind as well as the flesh. Make a separate attack roll for each head.

  • First head: 1d6 damage to both hp and INT.
  • Second head: 1d6 damage to both hp and WIS.
  • Third head: 1d6 damage to both hp and CHA.

Due to its size and weight, humans can only wield it two-handed, and must have at least 13 STR or suffer -2 to attack and damage rolls.

Carpet of Levitation

Value: 1500gp

An unbelievably filthy brown carpet woven with a yellow magic sigil. A creature standing on it may mentally direct it to move vertically up or down up to 20ft per round. It can hold up to four creatures and their equipment. Magic-Users studying the sigil may learn the spell Levitate from it as though studying a spellbook.

Choker of Denial

Value: 500gp per charge

Charges: 3d4

A simple choker made of black silk. If worn it permanently fuses with the skin forming a tattoo around the neck. Each time the wearer would be magically paralysed, polymorphed, petrified, level drained, magically aged, or subjected to instant death effects (e.g. Death Spell), a charge is expended and the effect is prevented. Unfortunately, however, the wearer is cursed to be unable to gain XP from any source. The curse can be ended by expending all charges or using Remove Curse, although the tattoo remains. Unlike most cursed items the curse is easily detectable by anyone trained in magic item identification.

Airblight

The sufferer hiccups constantly, negating attempts at surprise. After 1d4 days, the sufferer's body becomes comically overinflated. They float on water and are immune to falling damage. After a further 2d4 days, their chest pops like an overinflated party balloon, causing instant death.

Blood-Lunacy

The sufferer becomes bellicose and irritable. They become immune to fear effects, but cannot voluntarily leave combat and, after combat ends, must continue fighting any creatures present for a further 1d4 rounds. This can be negated by spending one point of CHA each time. They lose 1d3 CHA per day, and upon reaching zero CHA, die screaming, flailing, and frothing from the mouth. Any creature exposed to the sufferer's body fluids must save vs death or also contract blood-lunacy. If cured, only half the CHA lost (rounded down) is recovered.

Marionette Syndrome

Once per day at a time chosen by the referee, the sufferer must save vs paralysis or suffer an episode of marionette syndrome. During an episode, their body is jerkily piloted by the disease to attempt to spread the condition to others by scratching or biting them. Every time the sufferer experiences an episode, they suffer one point of DEX damage that persists even if cured, and they take a cumulative -1 penalty to further saves against the disease. If they reach zero DEX they die spasming and contorted. The disease is disturbingly semi-intelligent and will choose to strike at the worst times, for example when around vulnerable or sleeping creatures and simultaneously with other sufferers.

Meatrot

The sufferer loses one point of CON, which persists even if the disease is cured. A random body part (below) is swollen and blistered and cannot be used. After 1d10 days, the affected body part necroses and falls off.

Saintspores

The sufferer's entire body glows faintly. After 1d6 days, their entire body instead begins to glow as Continual Light. They become immune to pain and recover 1HP per round. After a further 1d3 days, their entire body abruptly crumbles into glowing dust. Any creature inhaling the dust must save vs death or be infected with saintspores.

Worm of Despair

The sufferer constantly feels on the verge of tears. On failing any roll, they are incapacitated weeping for one turn. The sufferer dies peacefully in their sleep after 2d6 days, at which point the worm burrows out of their brain and climbs up the nose of a random nearby sleeping creature (if any), infecting them without a save.
