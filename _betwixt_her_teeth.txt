<h1>Betwixt Her Teeth</h1>

<div>

<p>The cannibal cult of the Yearning Maw was wiped out fifty years ago, but her hunger survived. In the years since, the gluttonous, the decadent, and the spiritually hungry have found themselves drawn to her abandoned temple one by one. A new cult is taking form, its adherants unnaturally transformed, and with the region now in the grip of a merciless famine, violence and desperation loom inevitable.</p>

<p><i>Betwixt Her Teeth</i> is a forty room dungeon centred around a temple to the Yearning Maw, forbidden goddess of cannibalism and decadence, intended for characters around level 2-3.</p>

<h2><a id="introduction">Introduction</a></h2>

<p><strong>Adventure hook</strong>: you are actively starving to death. Get food or money to pay for food or die.</p>

<p><strong>Character creation</strong>: recommended level 2-3. Start at one stage of <a href="#starvation">starvation</a>. Cannot choose rations as starting equipment.</p>

<p><strong>Starting information</strong> easily obtained by asking around town:</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Famine</strong>: ongoing for months, everyone is within weeks or days of starving.</li>
	<li><strong>Tower of Misfortune</strong>: cursed wizard tower to the north (approx. 1 mile/2 turns walk).</li>
	<li><strong>Dungeon</strong>: temple to an evil god under mesa to the west (know directions to <a href="#main_entrance">Main Entrance</a>; approx 6 miles/12 turns walk), worshippers wiped out by knights fifty years ago, abandoned ever since.</li>
	<li><strong>Missing villagers</strong>: rancher, Pine, and son, Cob, missing for two weeks. Didn't get along well.</li>
	<li><strong>Other adventurers</strong>: several travelled to dungeon recently looking for money for food, none returned.</li>

</ul>

<h2><a id="spare_rib">Spare Rib</a></h2>

<p><strong>Famine</strong>: everyone except Mr. and Mrs. Hearty within days of starving to death.</p>

<p><strong>Remote</strong>: nearest significant settlement is three days' travel to northeast.</p>

<p><strong>Houses</strong>: miserable half-collapsed wooden buildings, fewer than half still inhabited.</p>

<p><strong>Children and infirm</strong>: mostly evacuated or already dead.</p>

<p><strong>Livestock and pets</strong>: all already slaughtered for food.</p>

<p><strong>Water</strong>: well has dried up, but <a href="#sister_dorothy">Sister Dorothy</a> casts <i><a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Create_Water">Create Water</a></i> daily.</p>



<h3><a id="buxom_sow"></a>The Buxom Sow</h3>

<p><strong>Dilapidated tavern</strong>: no longer operating, nothing to sell.</p>

<p><strong>Sign outside</strong>: disturbing illustration of laughing pig-headed woman.</p>

<p><strong>Corpses</strong>: four corpses lying under sheets, villagers who died of starvation.</p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Unburied</strong>: everyone is too physically weak to dig a grave or build a pyre.</li>
	<li><strong>Cannibalised</strong>: look intact at a glance, but <a href="#rodger">Rodger</a> has secretly removed and eaten the tongues and other soft tissues.</li>
</ul>

<h3><a id="general_store"></a>General Store</h3>

<p><strong>Conspicuously well-constructed</strong>: stone foundations, tiled roof, tasteful decorative accents</p>

<p><strong>Owners</strong>: Mr. and Mrs. Hearty.</p>

<p><strong>Sells</strong> general supplies useful to farmers/ranchers: 5 50ft ropes, various tools, pots and pans, hammers, knives, 3 hatchets, 10 oil flasks, 30 torches, 2 lanterns, wood, cloth, leather, sacks and bags, 3 hunting traps, 1 shortbow and 40 arrows.</p>

<p><strong>Food</strong>: has 50 preserved rations for sale at ten times normal price, price quintuples for every ten sold.</p>

<p><strong>Buys</strong>: jewellery and gems at 80% price, any other valuables at 50% price.</p>

<p><strong>Defense</strong>: Mr. Hearty wears chainmail under clothes, and keeps crossbow and vial of soporific gas (all in 10ft radius save vs death or fall unconscious for one turn) under counter.</p>

<p><strong>Basement</strong>: entrance in back room, hidden under rug, covered by coffee table.</p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Locked</strong>: heavy steel door, sealed with <a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Wizard_Lock"><i>Wizard Lock</i></a>, password known only by Mr. Hearty ("A job well done").</li>
	<li><strong>Contents</strong>: 150 preserved rations and 150 pints fresh water in barrels, chest containing 8,000gp in coins.</li>
</ul>

<h3><a id="misfortune">Misfortune</a></h3>

<p><strong>10th level <a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Magic-User">Magic-User</a></strong> obsessed with curses and cursed items.</p>

<p><strong>Currently trapped in demiplane</strong>: bodily processes suspended, does not need to eat or drink, can only communicate via familiar.</p>

<p><strong>Familiar</strong>: giant anteater named Toast.</p>

<p><strong><a href="#languages">Languages</a></strong>: Common Imperial, Esoteric.</p>

<p><strong>Trading</strong>: can give/receive items by making Toast eat/regurgitate them.</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Buys</strong>: any magic items at full price, <a href="#clay_curse_tablets">clay curse tablets</a> for 500gp each.</li>
	<li><strong>Sells</strong>: cursed scrolls below. Knows they are cursed but not how.</li>

<h3><a id="architecture">Architecture</a></h3>

<p><strong>Polished sandstone</strong>: yellow-orange-brown, visible streaks and strata.</p>

<p><strong>Organic style</strong>: rounded walls and corners.</p>

<p><strong>Ceilings</strong>:</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Corridors</strong>: 10ft high.</li>
	<li><strong>Rooms less than 30ft across</strong>: 20ft high.</li>
	<li><strong>Rooms 30ft or more across</strong>: 30ft high.</li>
	
</ul>

<p><strong>Doors</strong>: stone banded with bronze, always open in direction of larger room, blancmanges can squeeze through gap between door and frame in one turn.</p>



<h3><a id="cannibalism">Cannibalism</a></h3>

<p><strong>Butchering a person</strong>: yields twelve rations of edible meat.</p>

<p><strong>Lawful clerics and paladins</strong> knowingly committing cannibalism: lose all powers until atoned for.</p>

<p><strong>After death</strong>: anyone who has committed cannibalism and not atoned rises again as a <a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Ghoul">Ghoul</a> 1d6 days after death.</p>



<h3><a id="clay_curse_tablets">Clay Curse Tablets</a></h3>

<p><strong>Square clay tablets</strong> inscribed with ominous sigils.</p>

<p><strong>To use</strong>: shatter against ground, black smoke emerges from shards, nearest creature inhaling smoke must save vs spells or be afflicted by curse.</p>

<p><strong>Throwing into melee</strong>: affects a random target.</p>



<h3><a id="eastern_cavern">The Eastern Cavern</a></h3>

<p><strong>Thick subterranean foliage</strong>: giant terrestrial coral, giant lichens, patches of bioluinescent cave moss.</p>

<p><strong>Fauna</strong>: alive with small insects, amphibians, lizards, etc., constant buzzing, chirruping, squeaking, rustling, skittering noises.</p>



<h3><a id="history">History</a></h3>

<p><strong>500 years ago</strong>: shrine constructed to minor god of butchery and funerary rites, comprising <a href="#ruined_shrine">Ruined Shrine</a>, <a href="#zombie_philosophy_club">Zombie Philosophy Club</a>, <a href="#coffin_roulette">Coffin Roulette</a>, and <a href="#hungry_kitchen">Hungry Kitchen</a> (a mortuary at the time).</p>

<p><strong>200 years ago</strong>: worshippers of the Yearning Maw invade, slaughter, and eat worshippers. Rest of the temple complex constructed over the following thirty years.</p>

<p><strong>90 years ago</strong>: <a href="#spare_rib">Spare Rib</a> is founded nearby.</p>

<p><strong>50 years ago</strong>: in response to disappearances of cattle, and even people, a band of knights arrive and lay waste to the temple. They sustain <a href="#empty_graveyard">heavy losses</a> but all cultists are successfully slain or driven off.</p>

<p><strong>20 years ago</strong>: under divine guidance from the Yearning Maw, <a href="#mayflower">Mayflower</a> finds the abandoned temple and becomes the new high priestess. More cultists arrive over the following years and find themselves transformed into <a href="#mawfolk">Mawfolk</a>.</p>

<p><strong>2 years ago</strong>: <a href="#gartholomew">Gartholomew</a> prays to the Yearning Maw in desperation when cornered by Father Marmalade, a vampire hunter. She intervenes to save him, but forces him join the congregation of Mawfolk in return.</p>

<p><strong>9 months ago</strong>: a prolonged drought leads to an ongoing severe regional famine.</p>

<p><strong>3 weeks ago</strong>: Father Marmalade tracks <a href="#gartholomew">Gartholomew</a> to the temple, but is mortally wounded by Mawfolk and <a href="#marmalades_last_stand">perishes</a>.</p>



<h3><a id="languages">Languages</h3>

<p><strong>Suggestions only</strong>: substitute these with whatever is closest in your setting.</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Common Imperial</strong>: common language used by general population, spoken by almost everyone.</li>
	<li><strong>High Imperial</strong>: formal language used by aristocracy and knights, and the servants or squires who serve them, many aristocrats take pride in not needing to learn Common Imperial.</li>
	<li><strong>Esoteric</strong>: complicated language used for magical and highly technical texts, mostly written rather than spoken.</li>
	<li><strong>Ecclesiastic</strong>: semi-secret language developed by priesthood to protect divine mysteries from laity, commonly used for religious texts and prayers.</li>
	
</ul>

<h3><a id="the_river">The River</a></h3>

<p><strong>Flow</strong>: from northeast (<a href="#stepping_stones">Stepping Stones</a>) to southwest (<a href="#above_the_abyss">Above the Abyss</a>).</p>

<p><strong>Falling in</strong>: cold, deep, and fast, serious risk of drowning regardless of swimming ability, impossible to swim against current.</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Chance of drowning</strong>: unencumbered: 2-in-6; light encumbrance: 3-in-6; medium encumbrance: 4-in-6; heavy encumbrance: 5-in-6.</li>
	
</ul>

<p><strong>Drinking</strong>: safe to drink, no risk of disease.</p>

<p><strong>Fauna</strong>: teeming with strange transparent crustaceans and small bony fish. 

<p><strong>Fishing</strong>: the fish stock is already overexploited by the mawfolk and on the verge of collapse.</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Overfishing</strong>: if fishing even more, catch 1d12 rations of fish per day for 1d6 days, and then the stock collapses.</li>
	<li><strong>Stock collapse</strong>: can only catch 1d2 rations of fish per day. Mawfolk will run out of food and resort to extreme measures (e.g. attacking <a href="#spare_rib">Spare Rib</a>) after one day.</li>
	
</ul>



<h3><a id="starvation">Starvation</h3>

<p><strong>For each day without food, choose one</strong> to represent where remaining energy is least focused. Effects are cumulative. Cannot choose duplicates.</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Slow</strong>: Movement speed halved.</li>
	<li><strong>Weak</strong>: -2 to attack and damage, spellcasters prepare spells as though one level lower.</li>
	<li><strong>Tired</strong>: penalty to all non-combat rolls; -5 to d20 rolls, -2 to n-in-6 rolls, -25% to d100 rolls.</li>
	<li><strong>Irritable</strong>: roll 2d4 instead of 2d6 for reaction rolls.</li>
	<li><strong>Dead</strong></li>

</ul>
