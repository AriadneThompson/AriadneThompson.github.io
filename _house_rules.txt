<h1>House Rules</h1>



<div>

<p>This is a list of house rules for the <a href="https://necroticgnome.com/pages/about-old-school-essentials">Old School Essentials</a> game I run. It's mainly here for me and my players to reference, but you might find some you'd like to borrow for your home game!</p>

</div>



<div>

<h2>Classes</h2>

<p><strong>Permitted classes</strong>: no demihumans, all other classes allowed. (<i>Demihumans don't exist in my setting, plus most of them are just variants of the base human classes, and I'm not a fan of how infravision makes darkness less of a hazard</i>).</p>

<p><strong>Thieves</strong>: use the d6 Thief Skills alternate rules from <a href="https://necroticgnome.com/products/carcass-crawler-issue-1">Carcass Crawler Issue 1</a>.</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Read Languages</strong>: replaced with Detect Construction Tricks. (<i>This skill is otherwise only available to demihumans, and feels more thematically appropriate</i>).</li>
	
</ul>

<p><strong>Acrobats and Assassins</strong>: use d6 skills similar to Thieves.</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Skill points</strong>: start with three points, gain one point per level except levels 5, 10, 14.</li>
	<li><strong>Falling (Acrobats)</strong>: is not rolled as a skill.</li>
	<li><strong>Tightrope walking (Acrobats)</strong>: replaced with Gymnastics, a general skill for anything involving balance or contortion, e.g. crossing slippery surfaces or fitting through tight spaces. (<i>This skill is otherwise bizarrely specific and useless</i>).</li>
	
</ul>

<p><strong>Bards</strong>: use the Illusionist spell list instead of the Druid spell list, and use a spellbook instead of a sprig of mistletoe. (<i>Feels more thematically appropriate</i>).</p>

<p><strong>Illusionists, Magic-Users, and Bards</strong>: use the Advanced Spellbook Rules.</p>

</div>



<div>

<h2>Combat</h2>

<p><strong>Optional rules</strong> from the Combat chapter in the Player's Tome:</p>

<ul>

	<li><strong>Ascending Armour Class</strong></li>
	<li><strong>Variable Weapon Damage</strong></li>
	<li><strong>Attacking with Two Weapons</strong></li>
	<li><strong>Charging into Melee</strong></li>
	<li><strong>Parrying</strong></li>
	<li><strong>Subduing</strong></li>
	<li><strong>Morale</strong></li>
	
</ul>

<p><strong>Slow weapons</strong>: ignore the "Slow" quality. (<i>It makes combat a lot more annoying by adding another phase every round, makes two-handed weapons much less viable, and doesn't really add anything interesting to make up for it</i>).</p>

<p><strong>Bonuses and penalties</strong>: +2 or -2 to attack and damage if in an especially advantageous or disadvantageous position, e.g. flanking, high ground, surprise, invisible. (<i>Borrowed from <a href="https://whitehackrpg.wordpress.com/">Whitehack</a></i>).</p>

</div>



<div>

<h2>HP and damage</h2>

<p><strong>On zero HP</strong>: unconscious until you either gain or lose HP. (<i>This doesn't come up often, but it's nice to have something between being up and fighting and dying outright</i>).</p>

<p><strong>Damage reduction</strong> (e.g. monsters that take half damage): round down, but always deal minimum one damage on a successful attack.</p>

<div>

<h2>Hiring Retainers</h2>

<p><strong>Succeeds automatically</strong> but takes a variable length of time. (<i>Having to roll for every day of searching and make reaction rolls for every candidate takes a huge amount of time and doesn't really add anything</i>).</p>

<p><strong>Hiring a specific class</strong> (e.g. a Magic-User): spend 50gp per day for 1d10 days.</p>

<p><strong>Hiring a type of class</strong> (e.g. any martial class): spend 50gp per day for 1d8 days.</p>

<p><strong>Hiring anyone</strong> regardless of class: spend 50gp per day for 1d6 days.</p>

<p><strong>Hiring torchbearers</strong>: spend 10gp per day for 1d6 days.</p>

<p><strong>Wages</strong>: classed retainers expect a half share of treasure, torchbearers expect 1gp per day.</p>

</div>



<div>

<h2>Spells</h2>

<p><strong><a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Detect_Evil">Detect Evil</a></strong>: specifically detects demons and undead, or anything with demonic or undead magic/energies. Does not detect anything else. (<i>I don't want to decide what is evil or good, the players have to decide that for themselves</i>).</p>

<p><strong><a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Dispel_Magic">Dispel Magic</a></strong>: cannot end spells with Permanent duration unless the description explicitly says so, e.g. <a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Animate_Dead">Animate Dead</a>, Blindness/Deafness. (<i>Removing a curse should require Remove Curse, removing a quest should require Remove Quest, etc.</i>).</p>

<p><strong><a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Knock">Knock</a></strong>: additionally makes a loud noise, possibly alerting nearby monsters. (<i>Borrowed from later editions of D&D. Without some drawback, a third level Magic-User completely trivialises one of the Thief's most useful skills, which isn't much fun</i>).</p>

<p><strong><a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Know_Alignment">Know Alignment</a></strong>: give a very vague and general overview of the target's personality. (<i>I don't use alignment in my game</i>).</p>

<p><strong><a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Protection_from_Evil">Protection from Evil</a>/<a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Protection_from_Evil_10%E2%80%99_Radius">Protection from Evil 10' Radius</a></strong>: protects against any hostile creature. (<i>I don't use alignment in my game</i>).</p>

<p><strong><a href="https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/index.php/Reincarnation">Reincarnation</a></strong>: is a 5th level Druid spell, instead of a 6th level Magic-User spell. (<i>Healing and revival should be exclusive to divine magic in my opinion, Magic-Users are powerful enough already</i>).</p>

</div>
