January: Temple of the Yearning Maw

A temple dedicated to The Yearning Maw, goddess of gluttony and cannibalism.

Room Descriptions

1: Entrance Hall

Statue of an amorphous blob with human torso, arms, head emerging from the top. Anything organic placed in outstretched hands rapidly decays and turns to sludge. Plaque in front reads "Our Lady of Digestion".

Northwest wall has a mural of a wild boar's rotting flyblown corpse. Secret door behind its head. Northeast wall has a mural of an engorged snake with stag's head and antlers sticking out of its mouth. Secret door behind stag's head.

2: The Face

Wide semicircular mezzanine balcony over chasm. No railings. Small jutting bridge leads towards huge stone face with open mouth. Statues of a man in progressive stages of dismemberment (from centre westwards) or decomposition (from centre eastwards).

3: The Mouth

Fleshy walls, but as tough as stone. Any pressure on the tongue causes the mouth to close and anyone on the tongue to be swallowed into The Stomach (4). Scraping the back of the throat causes a flood of acidic vomit from The Stomach (4).

4: The Stomach

Acrid reeking pool of strong acid. Dissolves everything but metal, stone, and magical items, and does 3d6 damage/round to creatures. Entrance to The Guts (5) is 10ft below the surface.

5: The Guts

250ft long 5ft diameter fleshy tube. Giant tapeworm halfway down.

6: Drain Room

Rough hewn rock. Damp filthy floor. Iron grate in centre of floor covers a vertical shaft leading 60ft down to [ROOM ON LOWER FLOOR]. Pile of undigested items against one wall:

  • Mace of disarming: on hit, target must save or drop one item they are holding.
  • Flamberge of fireballs: casts Fire Ball (5th level) on hit, centred on itself.
  • Fine china figurine of a bipedal cat, worth 225gp.
  • 1850gp in loose coins.
  • Several sets of armour, swords, axes, polearms, hammers, iron spikes, lanterns, and pairs of glasses, all corroded beyond use.

7: Angel Feast

Steep staircase lined with pillars. Statue of toad-woman with ruby eyes (worth 1250gp each) at the top. Every 5ft climbed up staircase drains 2 points of STR. Can be prevented if out of view of statue (e.g. using invisibility, fully concealed head to toe, or covering/dislodging eyes).

Banquet table with rotting remains of feast behind statue. Seven sets of fine tableware worth 150gp each. Mural of baryonyx bowing its head to a woman in a black floral diadem on northern wall.

Eastern end of table has an angel carcass with three servings of unspoiled meat left on it. If eaten, instantly recover all HP, cure all diseases/poisons/curses, and gain enough XP to advance to next level. Invokes divine retribution:

1 Struck by lightning next time outside in rain (as 9th level Lightning Bolt spell)
2 Turn into a Werepangolin on full moons (12HD, AC as plate, 2x claws + 1x bite (1d8/1d8/1d8), immune to nonmagical unsilvered weapons, hostile to all life)
3 Rival adventuring party led by cleric two levels higher given divine quest to kill you
4 Turn into a Medusa. Happens over the course of one turn, with petrifying gaze only active once fully transformed.
5 Instiable hunger; require 10x as much food as normal, and any penalties for not eating enough are permanent, no matter how much you eat to make up for it
6 Instinctively distrusted by all animals and people. Must succeed on a roll even to be offered basic services (e.g. renting a room at an inn or buying things at a store). -2 to reaction rolls and retainer loyalty.

Additionally, clerics or paladins lose all powers until atoned for. Effects can be removed with a Remove Curse spell, but NPC clerics will instinctively recognise these are divine curses and may refuse to help.

8: Cube Trap

Illusory mosaic of toothy maw on floor. Covers a 15ft deep pit trap containing a gelatinous cube. Bottom of cube contains 145gp in coins, a sealed copper scroll case containing a scroll of Levitate, and the ruined remains of chainmail, a flail, three daggers, a shield, and a lantern.

9: Secret Dock

Reachable by 15ft ladder. A small dock with a circular boat. Boat has a winch connected by a rope to a bollard onshore. Unwinding the winch allows the boat to float downriver (west) and winding it pulls it back into the dock. Rope is old and frayed: unless replaced, 2-in-6 chance of snapping for every 20ft unwound.

10: Balcony

Balcony with stone balustrade overlooking river. Contains two plush armchairs and a chaise longue. Animal bones and empty bottles behind/under furniture. Locked stone door in north wall leads to Jail (11). Greater ghoul inside pounds at door if it hears noise from Balcony.

11: Jail

Greater ghoul attacks immediately if door is opened. Also in the room, two cannibalised corpses, torn apart with bones snapped open to get at marrow. Ghoul and one corpse are wearing enchanted wedding rings: +4 to saves vs charm or fear so long as both wearers are within 30ft of one another. Worth 1000gp each or 2500gp together.

12: Bench

An ornate wrought iron bench under a magic lamp (lit by Continual Light). Magic lamp can be removed and carried around. 20ft cliff to the south overlooks Fishing Grounds (14). 1-in-6 chance Chrysophage is here defecating. Either way, ground is covered in little lead pellets (chrysophage scat).

13: Chrysophage Nest

Hollow filled with sticks, shreds of cloth, etc. 5-in-6 chance Chrysophage is here.

14: Fishing Grounds

Shallower and gentler bend in the river, can safely wade in thigh deep without being swept away. Ground is littered with dry fish bones (snap loudly if stepped on). 3-in-6 chance that 1d12 mawfolk are here fishing. Independent 2-in-6 chance Quince is lurking offshore keeping an eye on the area. Reroll every hour.

15: Mawfolk Bridge

Unsteady wooden drawbridge spans river. Drawbridge is down if mawfolk are fishing in the Fishing Grounds (14), otherwise up. 1-in-6 chance Quince is lurking in the water below.

16: Stepping Stones

A span of rapids in the river. Several slimy rocks poking out of the water, just enough to be able to jump across. River is impossible to follow further north (ceiling too low and current too strong). 15ft waterfall immediately to the south.

17: Eastern Shore

Largely overgrown, only a faint path visible. To the northeast, a winding tunnel leads down to [ROOM ON LOWER LEVEL]. To the southeast, the path passes near to a Giant Terrestrial Sea Anemone. Beyond that, a path leads to the back of the Mawfolk Village (18).

18: Mawfolk Village

Piles of cushions and ruined soft furnishings, mixed with moss and animal bones. There is a smouldering campfire in the middle. Strong smell of rotting meat. All mawfolk not currently fishing or out hunting are here. Roughly one third are asleep at any given time, the rest are eating, socialising, repairing fishing nets, etc. See Mawfolk Society for further details. 2-in-6 chance Quince is here knitting or receiving a massage from Mayflower. 2-in-6 chance Gartholomew is here pacing back and forth or looking miserable. Locked door to the south leads to Gartholomew's Outer Sanctum (19).

19: Gartholomew's Outer Sanctum

Armchairs and four-poster bed with only slightly damaged furnishings. Bookshelf covered in romance novels. Ornate wooden desk with non-matching but equally ornate chair. On desk is an inkwell, quill, blank papers, and an enchanted jewelled letter opener (reads aloud letters opened, worth 1250gp). Top drawer contains letters from various eligible princes and princesses of nearby lands refusing offers of courtship. Middle and bottom drawers are filled with many drafts of Gartholomew's memoirs, all less than half finished.

2-in-6 chance Gartholomew is here staring at the ceiling or writing at his desk. Door to the northwest leading to Gartholomew's Inner Sanctum (20) is cemented shut and cannot be opened.

20: Gartholomew's Inner Sanctum

Door is locked, barred, spiked from the inside, and the edges are cemented over. Gartholomew can still enter and exit in gaseous form through the keyhole. There is a coffin containing grave dirt, a blanket, and a stuffed toy elephant (worth 10gp). 2-in-6 chance Gartholomew is here sleeping.

Gelatinous Cube in the corner of the room is kept in place by a thick line of salt on the floor, which it cannot willingly cross. Gartholomew keeps nondigestible valuables inside (he is immune to nonmagical damage so he can retrieve them safely). Contains floating inside: an iron chest containing 12,500gp in loose coins and a further 3500gp in gemstones, a +2 duelling rapier, and an enchanted platinum hand mirror in the shape of an open mouth (worth 885gp, safe and functional even for vampires, medusas, basilisks, etc.)

21: Baryonyx Room

Ornate hall lined with columns. Arched ceiling has enormous detailed mural of entrails, viscera, etc. Undead Baryonyx stalks back and forth, attacking anything nearby. Too large to fit into any of the doorways out of the room. Ground is littered with splintered remains of a large wooden table and chairs, tableware, and the shattered bones and equipment of at least three people.

22: Marmalade's Last Stand

Comfortable room with several plush chairs and end tables. Two jade ashtrays worth 50gp each. North wall has a mural of a rabbit being torn in half by two foxes (one half on either side of the double doors). North door is held shut with a heavy steel bar. East and west doors have chairs propped under the doorhandles.

Dead body of Sir Marmalade, a paladin, slumped across floor. Contents of backpack are scattered all over the floor. Searching reveals chainmail armour, kite shield, flail of smiting (+1; +3 vs undead or demons), gold and ivory holy symbol of Propitiation (goddess of honour, chastity and obedience; worth 350gp), a book of prayers, a garland of garlic, a wooden stake, a small hammer, 115gp in a coin purse, and a bundle of torches with the pitch sucked off (by the oil slime in [ROOM]). Trail of flammable oil leads south to 23.

Anybody touching the holy symbol is affected by the Quest spell, with the task of killing Gartholomew.

23: Oiled Bridge

5ft wide stone bridge. Wooden handrails have rotted and mostly fallen off. Oil slime in Storeroom (24) has left a trail of oil over the bridge making it extremely slippery.

24: Storeroom

Cluttered room. Toppled shelves and piles of crates and barrels. Several empty flasks of lantern oil toppled onto the floor. Floor is slick with oil. Oil Ooze lies in wait in northeast corner. Thorough search of the room reveals a crate of fine cigars (worth 1500gp) and two barrels of fairy elderflower wine (worth 900gp each). All contents of room are extremely flammable.

25: Cleaning Closet

Bench and line of pegs on western wall. Keyring (keys to Gartholomew's Inner Sanctum, Jail, locked drawer in Kitchen, and Lair of the Pomegranate Demon) and leather apron hanging from pegs. Mop, bucket, and giant 10ft wooden toothbrush propped against northeast corner. All items in room are acid resistant (but don't confer acid resistance to wearer/wielder).

26: Secret Shrine

Hidden shrine to The Whisper, an obscure minor goddess of secrecy and darkness. Room is covered by Continual Darkness and Silence spells. Statue of woman in a hooded cloak with a finger to her lips at northern end of room. Engraved into her cloak are various secrets (e.g. "I murdered my younger brother when I was thirteen and made it look like an accident", "I have been unfaithful to my husband with two different men and three different women").

An offering bowl in front of the statue contains 119gp in loose coins, a pressed false lotus (an extinct flower, worth 550gp) and an onyx bracelet with "for my daughter-to-be" inscribed on the inside in braille (grants wearer flight if in total darkness, worth 1600gp). If somebody steals from the offering bowl or defaces the statue, and is not perceived by anyone, they must save or be spirited away (disappear forever).

If somebody whispers a significant (referee's discretion) secret to the statue, it appears on her cloak, and they may automatically succeed a roll to move silently once. This only works once per person, no matter how many secrets are shared.

27: Clifftop

Unworked open cavern. Cliff edge leads 60ft vertically down into miles of churning underground river (certain death). River passes over a waterfall, dull rumble and water droplets fill the air. A hundred cave swallows (stats as Bats) roosting on ceiling. If disturbed by sudden movement (running or combat) or bright light (brighter than a torch), panic and flap around everywhere.

Small alcove under the lip of the waterfall conceals a waterproof satchel containing 1200gp in loose coins, a scroll of invisibility, and a wand of slime (turns organic targets into Green Slime; living targets may save to resist; 1d6 charges).

28: Catacombs

Unlike rest of dungeon, walls are made of angular, weathered clay bricks, and floor is loose flagstones covered in inch-thick dirt. Coffins and sarcophagi scattered around the floor or in small alcoves.

  1. A statue of a man trying to cover his eyes. Actually a petrified adventurer.
  2. Filled with bird bones (chicken, turkey, goose, etc.) and eggshells. One turn after being opened, a Skeletal Cockatrice animates itself (stats as Cockatrice, but only 2HD).
  3. Three Ghouls stuffed tightly inside, limbs intertwined. Require one full combat round to disentangle themselves and get up before they can attack.
  4. Contains another sarcophagus, which contains another sarcophagus, which contains another sarcophagus, which contains the skeleton of a pregnant cat. Opal encrusted collar worth 700gp.
  5. Mummified child. Loudly weeping Wraith attacks if corpse is disturbed.
  6. An Arcane Ooze. A wand, three magic scrolls and an enchanted sword lie underneath it, all completely drained of magic and useless.
  7. Two rats playing chess while a third rat watches from the side. Rats are intelligent but will pretend not to be. A dimly lit lantern and half-chewed lump of bread in one corner. Chess set is worth 95gp.
  8. Full of Green Slime. Underneath slime is a skeleton with a handaxe of muting (+1; on hit, target cannot speak for one turn. Wielder also cannot speak while held or for one turn after) in one hand and a roundshield of spell reflection (3-in-6 chance to reflect spells back on caster) in the other.
  9. Withered corpse with heavy lead stakes driven through all four limbs. If all stakes are removed, corpse animates and begins writhing around and screaming.
  10. A steep, narrow staircase leading down to [ROOM ON MUCH LOWER LEVEL].
  11. A single mummified tattooed foot wrapped in cloth. Functions as a wand of feather fall (1d12 charges).
  12. Full of maggots.

29: Kitchen

Walls are made of angular, weathered clay bricks, and floor is loose flagstones covered in inch-thick dirt. Meathooks hanging from ceiling. Large countertop with thick grooves worn into it (formerly a mortuary table). Cupboards and drawers line eastern wall. Large oven/range set into western wall (formerly a cremation furnace).

Set of nine viciously sharp butchery/flaying knives scattered around countertops (worth 25gp each). Searching cupboards and drawers reveals pots of saffron (worth 90gp), vanilla pods (worth 250gp) and grains of paradise (worth 170gp). Two Potions of Poison in a locked drawer.

When entering, and every turn spent inside, all creatures gain hunger as though they have not eaten in 24 hours. This effect can be dispelled if Bless or a similar spell is cast on the room.

30: Antechamber

Statue of grinning serpent-woman wearing a malachite and emerald periapt of indigestibility (any creature that bites you, swallows you whole, drinks your blood, etc. must save or die, incapacitated retching for 1d6 rounds on a success; worth 1600gp). If somebody's hand comes within 1ft of the statue it animates and attempts to bite it off. Save or take 1d10 damage and lose 1d6 fingers, losing entire hand on a roll of 5-6.

Southeast door leading to Baryonyx Room (21) is jammed but can be opened by force. North door to 31 is locked. Steady dripping and faint acrid smell caused by Grey Ooze lurking on ceiling above north door, will drop onto anyone below.

31: Lair of the Pomegranate Demon

Room filled with 3ft deep black soil, lit by pink Continual Light. Mural of a pack of hyenas ambushing a flock of cranes. Rooted in the centre is the Pomegranate Demon, surrounded by a magic circle made of powdered chalk. The magic circle is fake but the pomegranate demon will pretend it is bound and helpless to entice adventurers.

Set of shelves near the entrance has various gardening equipment (shears, trowel, etc.), some sodden illegible scrolls, and a clay curse tablet (summons 20ft radius swarm of biting insects, 1 damage per round, 10ft movement).

32: Crab Pit

Filled with 32 undead crabs (1HP, 1d2 damage, cling and cause a further 1 damage if torn off) and the skeletal remains of two people. Looting corpses yields 50gp in loose coins, a beryl necklace (worth 120gp) and a clay curse tablet (blindness + deafness).

Bestiary

Chrysophage

HD: 3

AC: as plate

ATT: 1x bite 1d4 or dazzling lights (1/day)

ML: 4.

Dazzling lights: all within 30ft save or be blinded and stunned. A golden-coloured giant beetle (size of a large dog) with stegosaurus-like plates running down its back and very large eyes. Makes a constant plaintive mewling sound audible from 30ft away, even while asleep. On sight, save or be charmed. Charmed creatures want to protect it and feed it all of their gold. May freely leave if they have no gold and don't believe it's in danger. Charm ends after 1d4 turns out of sight of Chrysophage. Stomach is filled with gemstones from gold jewelery it has eaten, worth 900gp total.

Giant Tapeworm

HD: 3, AC: as leather, ATT: 1x absorbing bite (1d6), ML: 4.

Absorbing bite: save or additionally take 1d6 damage to CON.
