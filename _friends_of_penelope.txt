<h1>Friends of Penelope</h1>



<div>

<p>Friends of Penelope is a Norfolk-based LGBT+ writing club founded in 2019. I am one of the founding members as well as the editor and typesetter of our anthologies (below). We meet online on Wednesdays at 6pm. If you're interested in joining, <a href="contact_me.html">contact me</a> for details!</p>

</div>



<div>

<h2><a href="Lost Ground.pdf">Lost Ground</a></h2>

<p><i>Lost Ground</i> was our first anthology, written in the aftermath of the 2019 general election. It features written submissions by Molly Potter, Amanda Coleman, Tony Warner, Andrew K Copeman, Aspen Perry, and Vanessa Atalanta, as well as artwork by Andrew K Copeman, Denzil Dean, and Anna Brass, and a foreword by Charlie Caine. The editor-in-chief was Andrew K Copeman, with additional editing and typesetting by Ariadne Thompson.</p>

</div>



<div>

<h2><a href="Friends of Penelope Volume One.pdf">Friends of Penelope Volume One</a></h2>

<p><i>Friends of Penelope Volume One</i> was the result of our first writing competition, held in December 2023, with submissions of poetry on any theme. The winner, selected by guest judge Molly Potter, was <i>Soft</i> by Dale Rune Cody, with <i>One Neat Trick for Ignoring Murdered Children</i> by Izzy Jarvis taking second place. Other submissions came from Andrew K Copeman, Dale Rune Cody, Ruadhán Ó Donnaile, Persephone von Sylvan, Vanessa Atalanta, and Theodora Medhurst. The front cover, editing, and typesetting was by Ariadne Thompson.</p>

</div>



<div>

<h2><a href="Friends of Penelope Volume Two.pdf">Friends of Penelope Volume Two</a></h2>

<p><i>Friends of Penelope Volume Two</i> was the result of our second writing competition, held in February 2025, with submissions of prose on any theme. The winner, selected by guest judge Lisa Mac, was <i>Reunion</i> by Ruadhán Ó Donnaile. Other submissions came from Andrew Karlyle, Nova Riddle, Eloise Blumenthal, Vanessa Atalanta, and Elspeth Rushbrook. The front cover, editing, and typesetting was by Ariadne Thompson.</p>

</div>



<div>

<h2><a href="Friends of Penelope Volume Three.pdf">Friends of Penelope Volume Three</a></h2>

<p><i>Friends of Penelope Volume Three</i> was the result of our third writing competition, held in February 2026, with submissions on the theme of "memory". The winner, selected by guest judge Anna Marie Clark, was <i>Remembering Us</i> by R. W. Maxwell, with <i>Haunting Henry</i> by the same author taking second place. Other submissions came from Andrew Karlyle, Barbie Gore, R. W. Maxwell, Mica Gillard, Nova Riddle, and Vanessa Atalanta. The front cover, editing, and typesetting was by Ariadne Thompson.</p>

</div>
